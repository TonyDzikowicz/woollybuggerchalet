The chalet

Your own stretch of the Betsie

Woolly Bugger Chalet is a cozy, private retreat tucked into the woods along the Betsie River. The whole property is yours: the chalet, the riverbank, the firepit and the private dock.

Inside, there's a living room with a fireplace, a fully equipped kitchen, WiFi, air conditioning, and a washer and dryer. Three bedrooms and a sofa bed sleep up to eight.

The chalet is named for the classic fly pattern. The Betsie is known for trout, steelhead and a legendary fall salmon run, so anglers can fish without getting in the car.
